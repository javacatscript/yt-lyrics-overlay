Lyrics Overlay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-18

Lyrics Overlay is a browser extension that shows real-time synced lyrics for whatever you're playing on YouTube or YouTube Music, in a window that floats above your other tabs and apps. This page explains what data the extension touches, where it goes, and why.

What the extension reads from the page

While a video is playing on YouTube or YouTube Music, the extension reads the track's title, artist, album (when available), and duration directly from the page. That's the only information it collects, and it's used for exactly one purpose: looking up matching lyrics.

The extension does not read your watch history, your account, your comments, or anything else on the page. It has no access to any site other than youtube.com and music.youtube.com.

What gets sent off your device

To look up lyrics, the track's artist, title, album, and duration are sent to the following third-party lyrics APIs. No account identifiers, browsing history, or any other personal data is included or attached.

Service What's sent When
LRCLIB Artist, title, album, duration First lookup for a track
lyrics.ovh Artist, title Only if LRCLIB has no match for that track

These are independent, free, keyless lyrics databases. The extension does not operate its own server and no data passes through any server run by the extension's developer — requests go straight from your browser to these APIs.

What's stored locally

The extension caches lyrics lookups and your settings (sync offset, overlay text size) using the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage). This data stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not synced to any account. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

What this extension does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties beyond the lyrics lookups above
  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles

Permissions this extension requests

Permission Why
storage Local caching of lyrics and your settings, described above.
sidePanel Shows the controls (sync offset, text size, plain-text lyrics) in the browser's side panel.
https://lrclib.net/* The primary lyrics lookup described above.
https://api.lyrics.ovh/* The fallback lyrics lookup described above, used only when LRCLIB has no match.
